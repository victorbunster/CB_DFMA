# Circular DfMA — definition

**Circular DfMA** is the integration of Design for Manufacture and Assembly with circular economy principles in building design.

It structures buildings as systems of independent, standardised, prefabricated components joined by reversible connections, so that assemblies can be disassembled without damage. The aim is to keep components, products and materials in use at their highest value — through adaptation, repair, reuse, remanufacture and recovery — across multiple service lives, while retaining DfMA's productivity benefits: off-site manufacture, reduced waste, rationalised interfaces and predictable assembly.

Key properties: component independence, connection reversibility, material separability, durability differentiation by layer, and information continuity.
